# CPL Student Portal — How Students Get Started

The **CPL Student Portal at [creditforbeingyou.org](https://creditforbeingyou.org)** is the
statewide, student-facing front door to Credit for Prior Learning. It is **free**, uses
**open sign-up**, and is open to **prospective students and enrolled students at all 119
California community colleges**. Its invitation to learners: *get credit for being you.*

Use this page to point a student to the right next step. The right step depends on the
student's situation — the portal is the primary path for most, and the per-college CPL
landing pages remain the right path for one specific case.

## What to do — by situation

### Exploring CPL, not enrolled yet, or want to see options across colleges → use the CPL Student Portal

This is the primary path for most students — especially anyone who is **not yet enrolled**,
or who wants to see what their prior learning is worth at **more than one college**.

1. **Create an account** at [creditforbeingyou.org](https://creditforbeingyou.org) and sign
   in. (The Home page also explains what CPL is and the kinds of prior learning that can
   count, before you make an account.)
2. **Add your prior learning in the CPL Builder** — enter whatever applies to you: military
   service (Joint Services Transcript / JST), industry certifications or licenses,
   standardized exam scores, language proficiency, and more. You only fill in the categories
   that fit your experience.
3. **Browse your matches** — as soon as you enter evidence, the portal matches you live
   against real CPL opportunities at colleges. Your **Dashboard** (also your landing page on
   every future login) shows matches grouped by college and, by default, sorted to surface
   the most and best matches first. Filters let you narrow the list.
4. **Submit for review** — for colleges accepting CPL review requests, one button sends your
   CPL information to that college for review.

> **Keep your personal contact information accurate in the portal.** After you submit a
> request, the college reviews it on their side and **contacts you directly** — so your
> details need to be correct and current.

Each page in the portal includes a short guided walkthrough, so you don't have to figure it
out on your own.

### Already enrolled and just need your college to review your documentation → use your college's CPL landing page

If you already attend a specific college and simply want that college to review CPL
documentation, go to your college's CPL landing page at
**[map.rccd.edu/cpllandingpages/](https://map.rccd.edu/cpllandingpages/)** and submit your
documents there. (This is a narrower case than the portal, but it's the right tool for it.)

### Your college isn't accepting CPL requests through the portal or a landing page → contact the college's counseling office

Not every college accepts CPL requests through the portal or a landing page yet. If that's
the case for your college, contact that college's **counseling office** and ask them to
review your prior learning for credit.

### Questions → email the MAP team

For anything else, email the MAP team at **[MAP@rccd.edu](mailto:MAP@rccd.edu)**.

## Good to know

- **CPL is never guaranteed.** The portal helps you discover and request CPL, but **each
  college makes the final decision** on what credit to award. Matches and guidance are a
  starting point, not a promise of credit — the portal itself carries these disclaimers, and
  they always apply.
- **It's free and open.** No cost, open sign-up, available to prospective students and
  students at every California community college.
- **JST upload is moving into the portal.** Veterans still upload their Joint Services
  Transcript (JST) for CPL — now through the CPL Builder on the Student Portal, which is
  replacing the separate standalone JST upload tool over time. The per-college CPL landing
  pages remain available.
