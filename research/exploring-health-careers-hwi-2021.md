# Exploring Health Careers: What Do You Want To Be?

**Source document:** `ExploringHealthCareers_IEDRC.pdf` (80 pages, ~22 MB, largely image-based/scanned) — the PDF itself is not mirrored in this repo; see the [Health Workforce Initiative](https://ca-hwi.org/) for the published guide.

## Quick Summary

A health-careers exploration guide edited by Valerie A. Fisher, RN, MA (Central Region Deputy Sector Navigator, Health Workforce Initiative — ca-hwi.org), produced by the California Community Colleges' Health Workforce Initiative (original PDF dates to 2021). This copy was circulated via the Inland Empire/Desert Regional Consortium (IEDRC) in July 2026. The guide maps entry-to-advanced **career ladders** across the major allied health and nursing fields — for each field it shows the progression of roles, education levels, and licensure/certification steps.

## Career ladders covered

1. **Nursing** — Patient Care Assistant → CNA → Home Health Aide → LVN → RN → Advanced Practice Nurse
2. **Laboratory Sciences** — Laboratory Clerk → Phlebotomist → Medical Laboratory Technician → Clinical Laboratory Scientist
3. **Physical Therapy** — PT/Rehabilitation Aide → PTA → Bachelor's options → Physical Therapist
4. **Occupational Therapy** — Occupational Aide → OTA → Bachelor's options → Occupational Therapist
5. **Pharmacy Services** — Pharmacy Clerk → Pharmacy Technician → Bachelor's options → Pharmacist
6. **Speech Language Pathology** — Rehabilitation & Audiology Aide → SLPA → BA Communication Sciences → SLP / Audiologist
7. **Emergency Medical Services** — Emergency Medical Responder → EMT → Paramedic → Firefighter/Paramedic
8. **Radiology Services** — Receptionist/Scheduler → Radiologic Technologist → specialty certifications (MRI, CT, Mammography, CI, DMS, PET) → Radiologist
9. **Respiratory Care Services** — Aide → CRT → RRT → specialty certifications → advanced degrees
10. **Dental Services** — Dental office roles and upward pathway

(Additional chapters continue through the remaining allied-health fields; the document is chapter-per-field, one career-ladder diagram plus role descriptions each.)

## Why This Matters

- **CPL health pathways**: The career-ladder structure maps directly onto stackable CPL opportunities — each rung (CNA, EMT, pharmacy tech, radiologic certifications) is a certification or license that faculty workgroups can articulate to course credit.
- **Faculty workgroup input**: Useful reference for the nursing and EMS discipline workgroups and for health-sector credit recommendations (EMT/EMS statewide recommendations already adopted by fire-pathway colleges).
- **Partnership context**: Complements the Futuro Health and Public Works Alliance EMS Corps partnerships (healthcare workforce → CPL pathways → baccalaureate options).
- **Counselor-facing**: A student-friendly "what do you want to be" framing that pairs well with MAP CPL landing pages when advising health-careers students.
