# Minimum Qualifications Disciplines — categorized (19th Edition)

All 240 disciplines from the official Disciplines Index, categorized by which
minimum-qualifications list they sit on. Machine-readable companion:
`cpl-project-tracker/kb/reference/mq_sections.json` (surfaced as the 🎓/🔧
chip on the CPL dashboard's Common Subjects Reference).

## What the lists mean

The CCCCO **Minimum Qualifications Handbook (19th Edition)** organizes faculty
disciplines into two principal lists, plus special CCR provisions:

- **🎓 Master's list (75 disciplines)** — requires a master's degree in the
  discipline (or the handbook's listed equivalents for that row).
- **🔧 Experience list (156 disciplines)** — *"disciplines in which a
  master's degree is not generally expected or available"*: **any bachelor's
  degree + 2 years of professional experience, OR any associate degree +
  6 years of professional experience** (CCR §53404 formula).
- **🎓🔧 Both lists (8 disciplines)** — appear on both, area-dependent.
- **📋 Noncredit provisions** — noncredit instructor MQs per **CCR §53412**
  subsections (citizenship, ESL, health & safety, basic skills, home
  economics, older adults, parent education, **short-term vocational
  §53412(j)**), plus DSPS provisions per §53414.

## Why this matters for CPL

The discipline assigned to a common course determines **which faculty are
qualified to review and sign credit recommendations** for it (Title 5
discipline alignment). Re-labeling a course family from an experience-list or
noncredit discipline to a master's-list discipline shifts the implied
qualification pool — e.g., noncredit QuickBooks/payroll offerings sit
naturally under *Vocational* (noncredit short-term, §53412(j)) or *Office
Technologies* (🔧), while *Business* and *Business Education* are both 🎓
master's-list disciplines.
